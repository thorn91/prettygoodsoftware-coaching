Privacy Policy

Last updated September 1, 2026

Pretty Good Video Coaching does not collect, transmit, or share any of your data. Everything the app creates stays on your device.

What the app stores

The app saves the following only on your device, inside its own private storage:

  • Videos you record in the app or import from your photo library or files
  • Drawings and angle/range-of-motion measurements you add to those videos
  • Voice notes and narration you record
  • Titles, client names, and any notes you type
  • Thumbnail images generated from your videos

What the app sends

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no servers, and no network features. Your videos and notes are never uploaded anywhere by this app.

Analytics and tracking

The app contains no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs. Nothing about your usage is measured or reported.

Device permissions

The app asks for these permissions only to provide its core features, and uses them entirely on-device:

  • Camera, to record video clips for review
  • Microphone, to record audio with clips and to record voice notes
  • Photo Library, to import videos you choose

Sharing

If you use the app's export and share features, you choose where the exported video or report goes (for example Messages, Mail, or Files). At that point the file is handled by whatever app or service you selected, under its own privacy policy. The app itself does not send it anywhere.

Data retention and deletion

Your data lives on your device. Deleting a clip in the app deletes its video, drawings, and voice notes. Deleting the app removes all of its data.

Because the app stores its data in its own folder on your device, that data may be included in your device's normal iCloud or computer backups if you have backups enabled. Those backups are controlled by Apple and your device settings, not by this app.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same address with a new "last updated" date.
